# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Customers browsing a home-based micro bakery in Germantown, Tennessee, choosing cookies and desserts for themselves, gifts or events. They need clear product, pricing and inquiry information on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Introduce By the Boys Bakery, show real treats, explain ordering and help customers submit an inquiry. The website should feel like the same bakery as its packaging and market booth.

## Brand Personality

Warm, energetic, playful and approachable. Modern with subtle nostalgia; professional and premium without a corporate tone. This direction and its packaging applications were selected by the owner in the brand-design conversation.

## Anti-references

Avoid inconsistent generated lettering, substitute script logos, childish cookie illustrations, distressed vintage effects and decorative 3D graphics. The bitten-cookie illustration is omitted. Use the selected outlined logo artwork.

## Design Principles

- Make the brand recognizable across a cookie sticker, shipping box, booth and website.
- Keep one lettering system and one palette across applications.
- Make products, prices and ordering information easy to read.
- Keep the experience friendly and straightforward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The brand standards specify readable brown-on-cream and brown-on-coral text and cream-on-brown reverse text. Coral and cream do not have enough contrast for normal text. Preserve keyboard focus, meaningful status colors, minimum 44px touch targets and reduced-motion support.
