# By the Boys Bakery brand standards

Version 1.0 / Review edition / 9 November 2026

This is the written source for the visual direction selected in the conversation and the recommended rules in the accompanying guide. Adopt these usage rules after reviewing the guide. Product dimensions, print stocks and supplier production specifications remain job-specific.

## Brand foundation

Warm, energetic, playful and approachable. Modern with subtle nostalgia. Professional and premium without a corporate tone. Fun without childish graphics. Write the name as By the Boys Bakery. Use clear, welcoming copy and verify product, price and ordering details before publication.

## Logo system

Use the provided outlined artwork. Primary for boxes, banners and the main signature; horizontal for tape, navigation and narrow formats; circular badge for stickers; BTB monogram for secondary stamps and sparse repeats. Never retype the brand name in Pacifico or another script. Never stretch, recolor arbitrarily, change word spacing or combine inconsistent lettering. Cookie illustration is omitted in Version 1.0.

The default mark is chocolate brown with coral accents on cream. The main circular badge keeps a cream field, brown lettering and coral outline. Chocolate one-ink and cream reversed versions are supplied. Use cream reversed artwork on chocolate backgrounds. Prefer a cream label on kraft for consistent contrast; direct brown printing on kraft needs a stock proof.

## Clear space and minimum size

Leave at least one BAKERY capital height around the visible full-name artwork. For the badge, use the internal BAKERY height. For the monogram, use one quarter of its B height. Keep at least one BAKERY cap-height between the descriptor and the first checkerboard square. Apply that margin to the whole mark rather than separating its internal elements.

Starting minimum widths: primary 32 mm / 160 px; horizontal 55 mm / 240 px; badge 38 mm outside diameter / 160 px; monogram 16 mm / 64 px. These are working limits, not results of a stock-specific physical print test. Enlarge when needed after an actual-size proof.

## Color

| Color | HEX | RGB | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warm cream | #FFF3E3 | 255, 243, 227 | Main background |
| Chocolate brown | #4B2418 | 75, 36, 24 | Logos and readable text |
| Primary coral | #EF7858 | 239, 120, 88 | Accents, checkerboard and buttons |
| Golden accent | #F4B775 | 244, 183, 117 | Optional detail |

Computed contrast: brown/cream 12.26:1; brown/coral 4.79:1; brown/gold 7.59:1; cream/coral 2.56:1. Use brown on cream and brown on coral for normal digital text. Use cream on brown for reverse text. Keep cream/coral for decorative combinations. WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 uses 4.5:1 as the minimum for normal text. Reference: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html

RGB values define the source palette. The supplier handles production CMYK or spot-ink conversion for the chosen stock and profile, with a physical proof. No universal print conversion is assigned here.

## Typography

The logo is outlined vector artwork, not a font. Nunito 900 for headlines, 700 for labels and buttons, 400 for body copy; 600 is optional emphasis. Use sentence case, clear hierarchy, flush-left paragraphs and body line spacing around 1.4-1.6. The kit includes Nunito 400, 700 and 900. Source additional 600-weight font software from Google Fonts if that optional weight is needed.

The booth HTML uses Nunito throughout. Editable PDF text fields use standard Helvetica fonts for compatibility. The logo artwork is the same in every template. Nunito's font license is included.

## Graphic elements

Coral-and-cream checkerboard with equal, undistorted squares. Supplied round-ended rays and underlines. Rounded buttons and panels. Keep illustrations, extra scripts, distressed effects, gradients and 3D graphic effects out of the current system. Use sparse repeats so the packaging stays clear.

## Shipping box

Use a primary logo on a cream panel or label, a cream circular badge sticker, and tape repeating the horizontal logo at even intervals. The selected sample uses cream tape with brown lettering and coral accents. Coral tape with brown lettering is an alternate. Keep marks away from folds, seams and label information. Supplier sets the box size, tape width and cut lines.

## Individual cookies

One cookie per clear bag. Put the cream circular badge on the front middle: centered left/right on the bag, vertically on the visible cookie face, below the closure. Leave film visible around it. Start at 38 mm or larger outside diameter and confirm the exact diameter on the actual bag and cookie.

## Booth

Use the letter-size menu and order/follow sheets together. Keep flavor names and prices prominent. Replace all bracketed values. Add one real QR code linking to the intended order or information page. The HTML editor accepts a QR image. It does not update the separate PDF snapshots. Print US Letter at Actual size, with browser headers/footers off. Large banners stay cream with the brown logo; tablecloths can use larger checks.

## Digital and photography

Horizontal SVG in navigation; Nunito for interface copy. Brown text on cream, brown text on coral buttons and cream text on brown hover states. Button radius 12 px, card radius 20 px, default brand border width 1.5 px, no decorative hard shadows. Functional and semantic states retain their own meaning.

Use actual cookies and ingredients, natural light and warm simple surfaces. Generated packaging images are concept references, not logo or print masters. The repository integration uses src/styles/tokens.css as the runtime source of truth and maps CSS and Tailwind styles to it. Outlined logo masters live in public/brand. The PDF review edition predates this integration.

## Handoff and versioning

Owner: By the Boys Bakery. Keep this versioned kit as the current source, with old releases archived separately. Change the master artwork first, regenerate all related formats, record the date and reason, and share the current guide and relevant vectors with suppliers. SVG is the editing master; logo PDFs preserve vectors; PNGs are screen exports. Label and packaging artwork still needs the supplier's physical dimensions, cut lines, bleed and ink proof.

The release manifest records SHA-256 hashes of all included files so the kit can be checked for consistency. The logo files are copied unchanged from the previously refined identity set.
