# Booth and packaging samples

The booth pages use the approved outlined primary logo and Nunito supporting type, with chocolate lettering on warm cream and coral checkerboard details.

- `booth-sheets-editable.pdf`: two US Letter pages with 15 editable text fields.
- `booth-menu-editable.pdf`: menu with four flavors, prices, descriptions, and a box offer.
- `booth-order-and-follow-editable.pdf`: order/follow sheet with contact fields. The QR area is a placeholder.
- `booth-sheets-editable.html`: self-contained source. Open in a browser, edit fields, and click the QR area to insert a real QR image. Print at 100% / Actual size on US Letter, with browser headers and footers disabled. Fields save locally in the browser when storage is available; the QR image must be reselected after reopening. The PDF files remain separate snapshots and do not update when the HTML is edited.

Replace all bracketed sample values and insert a real QR code before booth use. The checkerboard is inset 0.5 inch from the sides with a 0.33 inch bottom margin for ordinary home printing. Check printer margins at actual size. The PDFs are RGB drafts for tabletop holders, without bleed or crop marks.

The packaging PNGs are Canva's native previews. Open the linked generated images for the larger versions. These are concept renderings; the SVG master logos remain the print source.
